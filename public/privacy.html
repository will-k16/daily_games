Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

What we store

  • A random player ID in a cookie, so the game remembers your guesses and stats on this device.
  • Your guesses: what you typed, how the judge read it, and your scores.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and Google account ID. These are used only to keep your stats in sync across devices and to recognize the game's admins.
  • Reports you send when you think the judge got something wrong, including any note you write.
  • Your notebook, unit choice, and similar settings stay in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us.

What others see

Nobody else sees your name or email. Other players only see anonymous totals, like score percentiles and popular answers. An answer appears in "top answers" only after several players have given it, or after an admin approves it.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database.
  • Groq (and, as a backup, Google Gemini) receives the text of new answers so the AI judge can estimate them. Only the answer text is sent, never your ID or email.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose to use it.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts.

There are no ads, no tracking scripts, and we never sell or share your data.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, open an issue at github.com/will-k16/daily_games asking for removal. Don't post personal details there; we'll follow up. Clearing your browser's cookies also disconnects this device from its guest stats.
