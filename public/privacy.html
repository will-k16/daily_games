Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026. Covers every game on this site: Lifeline, Polly, Venn and Summit.

What we store

  • A random player ID in a cookie, so the games remember your plays and stats on this device. One ID covers every game.
  • What you play: in Summit, what you typed, how the judge read it, and your scores; in Venn, every answer you typed in a round, how the judge counted it, your round times and scores; in Polly, the side you picked (or that you skipped), your guesses and scores; in Lifeline, the name you typed for each round, who it turned out to be, and your scores.
  • If you sign in: your email address, plus your Google account ID (if you use Google) or a salted hash of your password (if you use one; we never store the password itself). These are used only to keep your stats, streaks, WK Bucks and pets' outfits in sync across devices and to recognize the site's admins.
  • WK Bucks: what you've bought with them for Skipper, Mel, Polly and Race (and Polly's town square), when, and the names you gave them.
  • Reports you send when you think a judge got something wrong, that a Polly question is wrong, confusing or unfair, or that a Lifeline person's dates are wrong, including any note you write.
  • Summit's notebook, unit choice and similar settings stay in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us.

What others see

Nobody else sees your name or email. Other players only see anonymous totals, like score percentiles, histograms and popular answers. In Summit, an answer appears in "top answers" only after several players have given it, or after an admin approves it. In Venn, "answers you missed" can include answers other players found, never who found them. In Polly, other players only ever see your side choice and guesses as totals (like "WK players" splits and score histograms), and only once 5 or more people have answered. In Lifeline, other players see which famous people were named for a round and how many times, never who named them. Nothing that compares players shows until at least 5 people have played.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database.
  • Google Gemini (and, as a backup, Groq) receive the text of new answers so the AI judges can check them: Summit's answers so the judge can look them up and estimate them, and Venn's answers along with that round's categories so the judge can decide if they fit. Only the answer text (and the puzzle) is sent, never your ID or email. In Polly, only the text of public poll pages is sent to the AI, and only by the site's admin; nothing you do in Polly is ever sent to the AI. In Lifeline, the AI only helps the admin write puzzles; nothing you type is sent to it.
  • Wikipedia and Wikidata receive the names you type in Lifeline (never your ID or email) so the game can look up birth and death dates. The animal photos in the pet corner's "Help your pets!" load from Wikimedia Commons, and its donation links go to each group's own site.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose to use it.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts.

There are no ads, no tracking scripts, and we never sell or share your data. WK Bucks are only for cosmetic items in these games; they can't be bought with money or cashed out.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, open an issue at github.com/will-k16/daily_games asking for removal. Don't post personal details there; we'll follow up. Clearing your browser's cookies also disconnects this device from its guest stats.
